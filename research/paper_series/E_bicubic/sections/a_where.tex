\paragraph{Format of the lower certificates.} A file \path{flow_r*.bin} starts with the eight bytes
\texttt{BICFLOW1}, then a 32-bit integer $r$ (the radius), then 64-bit integers $s_n$, $s_d$ (the switch
probability $s=s_n/s_d$ of the reference law), $n_A=4^r$ and $n_B=4^r$, followed by $n_A+n_B$ positive 64-bit
integers $X$ (all little-endian). Write $\mathrm{code}(w_1\ldots w_k)=\sum_i w_i2^{i-1}$, with the top symbol in the
lowest bit, and let $c$ be the parity of the window. The number $X_U(w)$, for windows with $U_1=1-c$, is entry
$c\,2^{2r-1}+\mathrm{code}(L_1\ldots L_r)\,2^{r-1}+\mathrm{code}(U_2\ldots U_r)$ of the first block, and $X_L(w)$, for
windows with $L_1=1-c$, is the same entry of the second block with $\mathrm{code}(L_2\ldots L_r)\,2^{r}+
\mathrm{code}(U_1\ldots U_r)$ in place of the middle terms. By Proposition~\ref{prop:rev} any positive $X$ gives a
valid flow, so a wrong reading of the format could only lower the bound, never invalidate it.
